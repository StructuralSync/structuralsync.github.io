Our Projects

Showcasing Our Expertise in Construction and Manufacturing

Roller Project - California

A $40K manufacturing project, the Roller Project involved producing high-quality rollers for a California-based client specializing in air conditioners and cooling units. MBC Contracting delivered the components with a 100% markup on materials, utilizing our Mexico-based machine shop for efficient production. The project included 20 hours of labor at 10,000 pesos, showcasing our ability to manage cost-effective, high-value manufacturing jobs across borders.

NJ Building

The NJ Building project focused on providing comprehensive construction solutions, including pricing estimates and material sourcing for fiber cement board (Hardie board) and stone wool insulation. Our team leveraged tools like SketchUp for 3D modeling and Microsoft Project for scheduling, ensuring precise planning and execution. Discussions with BuildPlannerPro highlighted environmental and synergy considerations, demonstrating our commitment to innovative project management.
