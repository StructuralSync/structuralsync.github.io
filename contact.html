About Us

MBC Contracting is a leading construction and contracting company specializing in innovative building solutions. From manufacturing high-quality components like fan guards, rollers, and aluminum exterior cases to sourcing premium materials such as fiber cement board and stone wool insulation, we deliver excellence for commercial, multi-family, and rental projects. With operations spanning Canada, the U.S., and Mexico, our team combines expertise, reliability, and global reach to bring your vision to life.

Our commitment to quality and efficiency drives every project, whether it’s a large-scale manufacturing job or a custom construction solution. We leverage advanced tools like SketchUp, Microsoft Project, and Bluebeam to ensure precision and collaboration, while our partnerships with trusted suppliers, including a dedicated machine shop in Mexico, enable us to deliver cost-effective results.

Our Services

  • Manufacturing & Supply: Custom components for air conditioners, cooling units, and more, with a focus on durability and performance.
  • Material Sourcing: High-quality fiber cement board, stone wool insulation, and millwork for diverse construction needs.
  • Project Management: Comprehensive planning and execution, from initial estimates to final delivery, using cutting-edge software.
  • International Operations: Seamless coordination across North America, with a strong manufacturing presence in Mexico.
