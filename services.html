Our Services

Comprehensive Construction Solutions for Your Projects

Manufacturing & Supply

We specialize in producing high-quality building components tailored to your needs. Our manufacturing capabilities include fan guards, rollers, and aluminum exterior cases for air conditioners and cooling units. With a focus on durability and performance, our products are ideal for commercial, multi-family, and rental properties. Partnering with our trusted machine shop in Mexico, we ensure cost-effective production with a 100% markup on materials for projects like our $40K roller job.

Material Sourcing

MBC Contracting sources premium materials to meet diverse construction requirements. We supply fiber cement board (Hardie board) for siding, stone wool insulation for thermal efficiency, and are expanding into millwork. Our partnerships with suppliers, including Turkish manufacturers like Vanucci and Lineadecor, allow us to offer flat-pack or assembled solutions, ensuring flexibility and quality for your projects.

Project Management

Our team delivers end-to-end project management, from initial estimates to final execution. We use advanced tools like Microsoft Project for scheduling, SketchUp for 3D modeling, and Bluebeam for document markups to ensure precision and collaboration. Whether it’s pricing for NJ Building or discussing synergies with BuildPlannerPro, we streamline processes to keep your project on track and within budget.

International Operations

With operations across Canada, the U.S., and Mexico, MBC Contracting offers seamless coordination for global projects. Our Mexico-based manufacturing hub, led by experienced partners like Daddyo from Chilliboys Manufacturing, enables us to deliver high-volume, high-quality components efficiently. From California to British Columbia, we make projects happen, rain or shine.
